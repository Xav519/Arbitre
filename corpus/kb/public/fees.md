# Fees and service charges

InnovaBank everyday chequing accounts have no monthly fee when the balance stays above 3,000 dollars. Below that balance the monthly fee is 4.95 dollars.

Outgoing wire transfers cost 15 dollars within Canada and 40 dollars internationally. Incoming wire transfers are free.

Interac e-Transfers are free up to 20 per month, then 1 dollar each.

A paper statement costs 2.50 dollars per month. Online statements are free.
