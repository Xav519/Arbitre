# Reporting fraud and contacting the bank

To report fraud or a lost card, call the InnovaBank fraud line at 1-800-555-0199. The line is open 24 hours a day, every day.

InnovaBank will never ask for a password, a one-time passcode or a payment to verify an account. Anyone asking for these is not the bank.

Secure messages can be sent from online banking at https://www.innovabank.example/messages.
