# Transfer limits and verification

Customers can transfer up to 1,000 dollars per request through the Inno virtual assistant. Larger transfers are completed in online banking or at a branch.

Transfers through Inno can only go to payees already registered on the account. Adding a new payee requires step-up verification in online banking with a one-time passcode.

The daily limit across all channels is 5,000 dollars for personal accounts.
